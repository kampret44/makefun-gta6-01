An original open-world coastal crime story set in Solvara. Play as courier Mara Vale: explore the sunlit waterfront on foot or in stolen cars, evade harbor patrols, and help a small crew uncover a customs ledger through five linked missions.

Take 2 re-recorded finalization: every counted file was revised in a recorded session and the four screenshots were re-captured from the running game, with the dossier verified before submit.

Take 3 re-recorded finalization: the menu camera now orbits the city instead of fighting the player camera, retries start cold with no inherited heat, the combat mission counts only kills made during it, and the four screenshots were re-captured from the running game, with the dossier verified before submit.

Serve the offline game with `node serve.ts` and open `http://localhost:8080`. Move with WASD, use E near a car, fire with F, strike with Space, and pause with P. Progress saves in local storage. All art is procedural and all audio is synthesized in the browser.
